# Roadmap

## What this is
A study task list for people learning a language. Each task is tagged with a skill, so a learner can see which skill they have been avoiding.

## What Done means
A stranger opens the live site, signs up with an email and password, adds a few study tasks each tagged with one of six skills (Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking, Vocabulary, Grammar), ticks off the ones they finish, and sees which skill has the fewest finished tasks. When they log out and come back later, their tasks and ticks are still there, and no other account can see them.

## Slices
1. Sign up and log in | done-criteria: a visitor with no account clicks "Sign up", enters a new email and password, clicks the link in the confirmation email, and sees their email address on the page with a "Log out" button; a signed-in person clicks "Log out", sees the "Log in" button again, and after reloading is still logged out; someone enters a registered email with the wrong password, sees an error message, and is not signed in; a signed-in person closes the tab, reopens the site address, and is still signed in | status: ACTIVE
2. Add tasks that stay | done-criteria: a signed-in person types a task, picks a skill from a list of exactly six, clicks "Add", and sees the task appear with its skill label; they log out, log back in, and see the same tasks with the same skill labels; a second account signed in on a different browser sees none of the first account's tasks | status: pending
3. Tick off tasks and see the avoided skill | done-criteria: a person ticks the checkbox next to a task, reloads, and the task still shows as done; a summary shows the count of done tasks for each of the six skills, and ticking a Listening task raises the Listening number by one; a person with done tasks in every skill except Speaking sees Speaking labeled "Most avoided" | status: pending

## Backlog
Editing tasks. Deleting tasks. Password reset. Google or other social login. Custom skills beyond the fixed six. Due dates. More than one language per account. History or charts over time. Streaks. Reminders. Sharing lists. Sorting and filtering. Dark mode. A phone app.
